MsS - Bottle Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the MsS - Bottle Puzzle mobile app (“the app”) handles when you play. The app is published by Makwana's Studio (“we”, “us”). In short: we run no servers, you do not need an account, and we do not collect your personal information ourselves.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your own phone, in the app’s local storage:

  • the player name you choose to type in (you can change it at any time);
  • the highest level you have reached in each liquid theme.

This information is never sent to us or to any third party by the app. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. You may see banner ads, full-screen ads between rounds, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch in return for a hint, an extra bottle, or an undo.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google does this under its own privacy policy and we do not receive this information. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and your ad choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the app shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it and before any ad is requested. If you decline, ads are limited according to your choice.

You can review or change your choice at any time with the Ad Choices link on the app’s start screen (it is shown where the law requires it). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone’s settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or store your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not collect precise location, and the app does not use your camera or microphone.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we do not sell your data.

5. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. There is no account to close.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California privacy law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data. We hold none about you; for data used by Google to show ads, use your Google account’s ad settings. You can contact us with any question.

8. Security

The app does not transmit your saved name or progress anywhere. Ads are delivered over secure connections by Google’s ad SDK.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
